Where the money goes

    What do these terms mean?
    Suggested amount to save
    The budget to aim for. It covers a higher-than-expected cost plus your safety buffer, so it is deliberately more than the average cost.
    Expected cost
    The most typical total for your trip, with no extra cushion added.
    Best case
    A low estimate. Only about 1 in 10 of the simulated outcomes came in cheaper than this.
    Likely range
    From the best case up to a pricier outcome. About 8 in 10 simulated outcomes fall inside it.
    Inflation
    How much prices rise each year. We use the destination’s past inflation to estimate how much more things will cost by the time you travel.
    Seasonal prices
    Hotels, flights and tours cost more in busy months and less in quiet ones, so your travel month changes the total.
    Safety buffer
    A percentage added on top for surprises like a missed bus, a rebooked flight or a doctor’s visit. Use 5% or less for a fixed, prepaid trip and 15% or more for a long or first-time trip.
    Travel style
    Shoestring, Budget and Comfort change how much you spend per day on lodging, food, transport and activities. Flights are not affected.
    Local transport
    Buses, trains, taxis and ferries inside the destination. Flights from home are counted separately.
    Flights
    A rough economy round-trip fare from your home city, based on the region you fly to. Real fares vary a lot, so check current prices.

    This is an estimate, not a quote. Costs, inflation and flight fares are approximate averages in US dollars, so real prices will vary. The range reflects inflation uncertainty only, not exchange rates or your own spending habits.
